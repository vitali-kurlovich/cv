
# AI and AI Transformation

Nowadays, IT companies are trying to apply internal process changes driven by the use of a new AI toolchain. Social media makes promises about dramatic improvements and reducing the cost of software development. But reality does not fit with reality.

If the question is: Can AI tools improve productivity and reduce the cost of software development? The answer will be: Yes. 

If the question is: Can AI tools worsen productivity and increase the cost of software development? The answer will be: Yes, too.

## Social Media

__If hammer sellers had invested tens of billions in promoting hammers as a tool capable of replacing the shovel, today we would see multitudes of people trying to dig a trench with a hammer.__

---

AI companies like OpenAI and Anthropic are investing a lot of money for promotes theirs products. Many of us have seen news that looks like science fiction.

One of the rules in mass marketing: If you want to sell something with a huge margin, you must sell a dream. So, OpenAI and Anthropic sell a dream about a magic black box that can replace a lot of developers - in an ideal - can replace all developers. 

On social media, a multitude of videos appeared, the aim of which was to promote many narratives.

Let's look at narratives closely:

 - Writing source code can be cheap if you use AI-generated tools: In some cases, it is really true. But let's see one example: UBER burned through its annual budget in a few months, turning tokens into code - code that nobody understands well.

 - Routine tasks do not need junior and middle engineers: The main role of junior and middle engineers was to implement about 80% of the functionality, enabling senior programmers to focus on the most complex parts of the system. AI tools can improve the productivity of middle engineers to a greater extent than senior developers. So, who was fired first? 

 - Source code quality: It doesn't matter anymore. - It looks more like an excuse for slop. The quality of the source code affects the quality of the final service; as a result, low quality can cost the company a lot of money.
